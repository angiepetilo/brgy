{% icon 'shield' cls='icon-primary' %} Data Privacy Notice

Compliance with Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012)

The Barangay is committed to protecting your personal data and privacy. By submitting your resident registration, you understand and consent to the following:

  • Collection: We collect your name, contact details, address, civil status, birthdate, and valid identification to verify residency and eligibility for barangay services.
  • Purpose: Your information is processed exclusively for official record-keeping, issuance of barangay certificates/clearances, emergency assistance, and public notices.
  • Protection: All documents and personal details are securely stored and accessed solely by authorized barangay personnel.
  • Rights: As a data subject, you hold the right to access, update, or request the correction of your personal information in our registry.

For questions regarding data privacy, you may contact the Barangay Secretary or Data Protection Officer directly at the Barangay Hall.
